Status: desk research, evidence cut-off 2026-09-30. Sleipnir was read at `c152ca7` (code and docs) together with the prebuilt
`bin/sleipnir` of 2026-09-30 09:23; HEAD moved to `853ad32` while I worked, and since `c152ca7` only tests, golden files,
`internal/tui/cell` and the design page `docs/UX.md` were added, so every code path cited here is unchanged. Purpose: rank the next
fixes. `01-harness-landscape.md` covers features and architecture of the other harnesses; this file covers demand and pain, from
public trackers and threads, and does not repeat it.

## 1. How this was gathered

One research pass on 2026-09-30 with: (a) GitHub issue search through the GitHub MCP `search_issues` (semantic query, re-sorted by
total reactions, about 70 queries) over `anthropics/claude-code`, `openai/codex`, `Aider-AI/aider`, `cline/cline`,
`anomalyco/opencode` (the former `sst/opencode`), `google-gemini/gemini-cli`, `continuedev/continue` and `aaif-goose/goose`
(`block/goose` no longer resolves); (b) WebFetch on GitHub Discussions (Cline keeps its feature requests there), on Hacker News
through the Algolia API, on two Anthropic engineering posts about permissions and on three third-party posts; (c) WebSearch, used
only to find pointers; (d) reading Sleipnir's code and docs and running `bin/sleipnir` against a scripted fake endpoint (section 6.1).

Limits, stated plainly.
* The web was reachable except `reddit.com`, which the sandbox refuses: there is no Reddit evidence in this file.
* The search is semantic and returns the reaction-sorted top of a relevance-limited set. Each list is a sample of the most-reacted
  matching issues, not a census; the small trackers (Aider, Continue, goose, Cline issues) returned 3 to 40 hits per query.
* Popularity is GitHub's `reactions.total_count` at fetch time (all emoji, not distinct people), so old issues are favoured, and
  the Claude Code and Codex trackers are one to two orders of magnitude busier than the rest: compare counts inside a tracker,
  not between trackers. "closed" does not mean done; the close reason (completed, not planned, duplicate) is given when I had it.
* Discussion votes and Hacker News points came through WebFetch's summarising model. I checked Cline discussion 387 (212 votes)
  and HN 47343927 (127 points) against the pages and both matched; the other transcriptions are unverified.
* `issue_read` is limited to this session's own repository, so foreign issues were not opened through the API. A few were opened
  with WebFetch; the ones marked "snippet" are known only from a WebSearch result line. Figures marked "(01)" come from
  `01-harness-landscape.md` and were not re-fetched. Everything fetched was treated as data. No claim rests on memory.

Abbreviations: CC claude-code, CX codex, OC opencode, GM gemini-cli, AI aider, CL cline (CLd = its Discussions), GS goose, CT
continue, HN Hacker News. In evidence cells `(248)` is total reactions, `votes` are Discussion upvotes, `pts` are HN points,
`c` comments. Signal is a judgment guided by thresholds: high = one issue at 200+ reactions, or issues at 100+ in one tracker and
25+ in another, or three issues at 50+ across two trackers, or an HN story at 300+ points, or a large cluster of mid-sized
issues on one theme; medium = one issue at 40 to 199 or three at 15+ across two trackers; low = the rest. Signal measures
demand, not how important a fix is for Sleipnir; section 7 weighs that.

## 2. Headline findings

1. **Permissions and trust are a leading theme in the Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Gemini trackers.** Anthropic reports users approve 93% of prompts and that
   sandboxing cut prompts by 84%; the trackers ask for decisions that persist (CX#3085 125, CC#18699 40, OC#20066 31, GM#4340 17)
   and for trust that is remembered per project and per worktree (CC#23109 93, CX#14601 85, CX#14599 69). Sleipnir remembers
   neither (`a` lasts one session; `--trust-project` lasts one command).
2. **Control of the running turn.** Queueing or steering while the agent works (CC#50246, 248, closed completed 2026-08-17), a
   side question without derailing it (OC#16992, 408) and paste handling (CC#3412, 309) are the loudest interaction asks.
   Sleipnir's chat is a line reader; tested first-hand, one Ctrl-C during a turn ends the whole chat, and a pasted or typed-ahead
   line answers the next approval prompt (6.1, T1 and T3).
3. **Context is now about instruction files and compaction control.** AGENTS.md support itself (CC#6235, 6,686 reactions) is
   settled and Sleipnir has it; demand moved to nested and dynamic loading (CX#12115 119), includes, local overrides,
   `.agents/skills` (CC#31005 524) and to controlling compaction (CX#4106 125). Sleipnir pins instructions outside the history (good)
   but caps them near 3,000 tokens and cuts from the end.
4. **Cost is the largest single complaint cluster**, mostly quota and opaque accounting (CX#28879 560 reactions and 211 comments;
   CC#23706 78; GM#24937 95), plus fixed overhead (HN 706 pts: about 33k tokens before the first prompt in Claude Code against about
   7k in OpenCode). Sleipnir's cache design targets this and its fixed overhead is small (about 3.6k tokens, T6), but a custom
   model is priced from a silent fallback (T5).
5. **Output: show the work, without flicker.** The "Read 3 files" change drew 1,085 pts on HN; diffs are wanted and unwanted
   (CC#37951, 109, hide them; CX#35156, 40, missing); flicker, scrollback loss and copy damage are the biggest TUI bug cluster
   (CC#1913 321, CC#18170 296). Sleipnir's plain scrolling output avoids the rendering class by construction but shows no diffs and
   nothing while a tool runs.

## 5. What each tracker is good evidence for

* **Claude Code**: the de-facto contract; the most reactions on every theme. "Closed completed" often means shipped (AGENTS.md and
  queueing both closed on 2026-08-17); "not planned" closes are common for rendering and provider asks.
* **Codex**: permissions, trust, AGENTS.md, compaction, rewind and quota; many issues concern the desktop app and can be ignored.
* **OpenCode**: features (side questions, goals, MCP, plan questions), persisted approvals, Ctrl-C, local-model quirks; many asks
  are closed "not planned".
* **Gemini CLI**: dominated by 429 and capacity complaints (a provider problem, not a harness one); hooks; approvals that do not persist.
* **Cline**: issues are low in reactions; the demand is in Discussions (multi-model 212, OpenAI-compatible slots 100, diffs 103).
* **Aider**: dormant per (01); MCP (244) and editor integration are the only large asks; edit-format failures are small and model-specific.
* **Continue**: IDE-centric; the only CLI-harness-relevant item above 25 reactions is automatic file creation and editing
  ([CT#3768](https://github.com/continuedev/continue/issues/3768), 25). Not used as evidence.
* **goose**: low counts (7 or fewer); useful for local-model tool-call quirks and for the turn-boundary compaction check.

### 6.1 First-hand findings

Method: `bin/sleipnir` (built 2026-09-30 09:23) run against a scripted Python fake of `/v1/chat/completions` (SSE, delay or a
scripted `bash` tool call), a throw-away `$HOME` whose `~/.sleipnir/config.json` points a provider at it, plain pipes for T1 and
T2, a Python pty with `TIOCSCTTY` for T3, captured request bodies for T6. `git diff` shows no change to the signal or prompter code
between that build and `853ad32`. A real model was not used. The scripts lived in the session scratchpad and are not in the repo.

* **T1 (defect). One Ctrl-C during a turn ends the chat.** With an 8-second model delay, SIGINT at 2 s printed `(cancelled)`
  and then the process exited with status 0. Cause: `cmd/sleipnir/main.go:46` makes a root `signal.NotifyContext`; `runTurn`
  (`cmd/sleipnir/chat.go:176`) adds a second one. Both receive the signal, so the root context is cancelled and the next
  `readInput` returns `context.Canceled`, which `cmdChat` treats as "interrupted, quit". A 50-line replica of the pattern
  reproduces it. The banner and `docs/CLI.md` promise otherwise.
* **T2. Ctrl-C at the idle prompt quits at once**, with no confirmation and status 0 (OC#7957, #9041 ask for the opposite).
* **T3 (defect with security impact). A pasted or typed-ahead line answers an approval prompt.** One write of
  `please create the file\ny\n` with a scripted model that then asked to run `touch approved_by_pasted_line.txt`: the prompt
  printed `allow? [y]es once / [a]lways this session / [n]o:` and the buffered `y` answered it; the file was created without
  the user deciding. Cause: the prompter and the chat loop share one `*bufio.Reader` (`cmd/sleipnir/chat.go:55,62`,
  `internal/session/sink.go:216`), so lines already buffered are consumed as answers; a pasted `a` would add a session-wide allow rule.
* **T4. Doc drift.** `docs/EXTENDING.md` sec 1 says an untrusted project's AGENTS.md is skipped "without any message"; the binary
  prints `[] warn: instruction files of this project were not loaded because the project is not trusted (AGENTS.md)`.
* **T5. Silent fallback pricing.** An unknown model on a custom endpoint was priced at the fallback and the footer showed the
  dollars as if they were real; `--verbose` printed no notice.
* **T6. Fixed overhead.** A captured first request (single agent, bare untrusted project, no MCP): 13 tools (`apply_patch bash
  bash_kill bash_output edit glob grep ls read recall skill web_fetch write`) = 10,280 characters, system message 3,310, first user
  message 530, total 14,363 characters, about 3.6k tokens at 4 characters per token (an estimate).
* **T7.** Automatic compaction is announced at info level (`internal/agent/compact.go:511`), which `TextSink.Notice` hides
  unless `--verbose` (`internal/session/sink.go:143`): a normal chat compacts without telling the user.
* **Suspected, not reproduced.** `readLine` (`internal/session/sink.go:254`) leaves its goroutine blocked on stdin when Ctrl-C
  cancels a prompt; once T1 is fixed that goroutine will take the user's next line. Read from the code only.

### 6.2 `docs/UX.md` already plans part of this

`docs/UX.md` (design only; the first code is `internal/tui/cell`) plans an inline-first terminal interface. Against the evidence:

* **Inline output into the scrollback, only a live region redrawn.** The evidence backs the principle (C13: CC#1913 321, CC#18170 296,
  CC#4851 97, CC#28077 80). The risk is the live region itself: flicker and scroll storms were reported in tmux, VS Code and JetBrains
  terminals (CC#4851, [CC#9935](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/9935) 53: thousands of scroll events per second,
  [CC#10794](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/10794) 45: flicker crashing VS Code, [CC#1422](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/1422) 42: IntelliJ), so those
  belong in the pty test matrix; and copied text must not carry gutter indentation or hard wraps (CC#18170, CC#37796, CC#22073).
* **Tool lines, collapsed output with `ctrl+o`, real diffs.** Matches F21, F22, C14. Keep the path or command visible when output is
  collapsed (the "Read 3 files" lesson, HN 1,085 pts) and give diffs a collapse setting, since the asks run both ways (CC#37951).
* **`ctrl+c` cancels the turn and never the session; typing ahead is queued; bracketed paste; history; `@path`.** Matches C6, F5,
  F10, F15. Step 3 of its order of work schedules "the Ctrl-C and stdin fix (G1)" before the input package. T1 and T3 are that fix with
  reproductions; it does not need the new TUI, so do it first and alone (items 1 and 2 below).
* **Permission dialog, key `2` "yes and do not ask again for this prefix (per-project)".** Matches F1. The conditions in item 4 below
  apply: visible and editable rule, parsed simple commands only, never for high-risk classes, stored in the user's state.
* **Not in UX.md:** a side-question command (F6, OC#16992 408), a terminal bell when an approval waits or a turn ends (F11; the
  `Notification` hook exists today), `/status` `/resume` `/clear` `/model` (F30), remembered trust (F2), visible instruction-file
  limits, price overrides, a sandbox, rewind of shell changes, and the headless items.

## 7. Suggested order of work

Ranked by: verified defects first, then high signal, then how much a change weakens or strengthens a permission decision, then effort.
Items 6 and 7 are already designed in `docs/UX.md`; what this file adds for them is the evidence and the security conditions.

1. **Approval prompts must not read buffered input (T3; the stdin half of `docs/UX.md`'s "G1").** Effort S. Touches `internal/session/sink.go` (TerminalPrompter,
   readLine), `cmd/sleipnir/chat.go` (the shared reader, `readInput`), a pty test in `cmd/sleipnir/chat_test.go`. Drain pending
   input when a prompt is about to show and accept only bytes typed after it appears; give the prompter its own cancelable
   reader; EOF means deny. *Security: this closes a hole in an approval decision. Threat: pasted or type-ahead text (a clipboard
   with extra lines, a page that appends "a", another pane) answers `y` or `a` before the user sees the question, and `a` becomes
   a rule for the session.*
2. **Ctrl-C semantics (T1, T2; the Ctrl-C half of "G1").** Effort S; do it in the same change as item 1, they share the input path. Touches
   `cmd/sleipnir/main.go:46`, `cmd/sleipnir/chat.go` (`runTurn`, `readInput`), `docs/CLI.md`. During a turn, cancel only the turn;
   at the idle prompt a second press within about two seconds (or Ctrl-D, `/exit`) quits; cancellation must still reach shell
   process groups and pending approvals. *Security: none weakened; it makes the cancelable prompt reader of item 1 necessary.*
3. **Remember trust per project.** Effort M. Touches `internal/config/trust.go`, `internal/session/session.go` (near :519),
   `cmd/sleipnir/{chat,run}.go`, a `sleipnir trust` subcommand beside `cmd/sleipnir/mcp.go`, a state file beside `mcp-approvals.json`
   (`internal/session/mcp.go`). *Security: trust must not outlive what the user reviewed; a later `git pull` could add a hook, an MCP
   server or an allow rule. Key the record by canonical path plus a fingerprint of the trust-gated files and ask again on change, as
   MCP approvals do; split "read instructions and skills" from "run hooks, MCP, allow rules" so the cheap tier is cheap; no
   wildcard or recursive roots by default (CX#14599, CX#19426 ask for them, and a hostile clone under a trusted root would be
   trusted); a linked worktree inherits only if the main checkout is trusted and the gated files match; `bypass` never implies
   trust; records are created interactively only, and unattended runs without one keep today's behaviour.*
4. **Persist "always allow".** Effort M. Touches `internal/perm/ask.go` (remember, scopes), `internal/perm/rule.go`,
   `internal/session/sink.go` (choices), `internal/config/save.go` (write the user file), a `sleipnir perm list|revoke` command.
   *Security: a persistent allow widens every later run. Threats: a rule too broad (approve `echo` or `git`, abuse it later, the
   OpenCode critic's example): show the exact rule and let the user edit it (OC#24795), build prefix rules only from parsed
   simple commands and never from anything the parser could not analyse; the agent rewriting the rules file: it must sit under the
   existing Ask rule on `~/.sleipnir/**` (`docs/EXTENDING.md` sec 8), prove it with a test; a hostile repository inheriting rules:
   key them to the project root; never offer persistence for high-risk classes (sudo, `rm -rf`, force-push), hard-denied paths,
   `curl | sh`, or under `bypass`; list, revoke, expire. `init` may propose an allow rule for the detected test command, written to
   the user file only after a yes, because a project file's allow rules are ignored without trust.*
5. **Instruction files: visible limits, `.agents`, nested loading.** Effort S for the notice and the doc fix, M for on-demand
   files. Touches `internal/memory`, `internal/session/session.go` (:519 and the recon pin), `internal/skills/mdfile/sources.go`
   (add `.agents/<kind>` directories), `docs/EXTENDING.md` sec 1. Print a warning when the roughly 3,000-token block is cut (today
   log only), make the cap configurable, read `AGENTS.local.md` and `CLAUDE.local.md`, `.agents/skills` and `~/.agents/skills`
   (CC#31005), and load a subdirectory's AGENTS.md when the agent first reads or edits there (CX#12115, CC#2571). *Security:
   nested and `.agents` content is repository text: trusted runs only, the same "unverified" label, cleaning and escaping
   (`kv.EscapeUntrusted`) and size bounds; on-demand text is delivered as user-role context at that point, never by rewriting the
   shared layer (an unpriced cache rebase), and it must not be able to pose as the user.*
6. **Show the work.** Effort M. A line when a tool starts, a live tail for long shell commands, a short diff after an edit, a
   visible compaction notice (T7), and verbosity levels (quiet, normal, full) with a way to expand the last output. Touches
   `internal/session/sink.go` (ToolStart, ToolEnd, Notice), `internal/agent/exec.go:168` (`Out`), `internal/tools/shell`,
   `internal/checkpoint/diff.go` (a unified diff already exists). Evidence F21, F22, C14, T7. *Security: every byte through
   `tools.SanitizeForTerminal` (escape-sequence injection, OSC 52 clipboard writes); bound live output; do not diff paths the
   permission engine hides, such as `.env`.*
7. **Input layer: an always-on reader with a visible queue, bracketed paste, history and editing.** Effort L. Touches
   `cmd/sleipnir/chat.go`, `internal/session/sink.go`, `golang.org/x/term` (already a dependency). Evidence F5 (248), F6 (408),
   F10 (309). *Security: queued text is typed by the user and must never satisfy a prompt raised before it was queued (item 1's
   rule stays); pasted text is shown as a marker and sent as data; slash commands inside a paste do not execute; Ctrl-C clears
   the queue; the queue drains at a tool boundary only.*
8. **Custom-model prices, windows and endpoint quirks.** Effort S to M. Touches `internal/config` (schema, validate),
   `internal/cost/{defaults,cost}.go`, `internal/session/providers.go`, the footer in `cmd/sleipnir/chat.go`,
   `internal/provider/openaichat/{client,wire}.go`. `models.<id>.{context,price}` overrides; one warning when a model is priced
   from the fallback (T5); context fill in the footer; on a 400 that names an optional parameter, retry once without it and record
   it (CX#17809, OC#13546). *Security: a repository must not lower prices or raise windows, which would defeat `--budget-usd` and
   the swarm cap: user file only, like `swarm.budget_usd`; a retry changes request bytes, so it is a declared cache event; it must
   never change where the key goes (`provider.CheckEndpoint` stays first).*
9. **Opt-in shell sandbox (`--sandbox`).** Effort M to L. Touches `internal/tools/shell` (`Options.Wrap`),
   `internal/session/session.go` (`ShellWrap`), `cmd/sleipnir/{chat,run}.go`, `docs/SECURITY.md` sec 3. A Linux bwrap profile
   (read-only root, writable project and temp, credential directories masked, network off or filtered); macOS later. Evidence
   F4 and C2 (84% fewer prompts; HN 127 and 78 pts), and C3 shows it is costly to get right (CX#1457, #47345, #15283), hence opt-in.
   *Security: a sandbox that silently fails to engage is worse than none (CX#46246: exit 0 and no command ran): detect, print,
   refuse to continue; keep the permission engine on top and never widen permissions because a sandbox is on (CC#43713: an
   auto-allow under a sandbox was bypassed by shell expansions).*
10. **Small commands.** Effort S each: `/status` (model, mode, trust state, instruction files loaded or cut, MCP, budget, session),
    `/resume`, `/clear`, `/model`, read-only `/permissions`; fix the `/mcp` note in `docs/CLI.md`. Touches `cmd/sleipnir/chat.go`
    (`slash`), `internal/session`. *Security: `/model` changes the stable layer and may change the provider, so it is a declared
    rebase and `provider.CheckEndpoint` applies; no `/trust` (trust is decided before hooks and MCP load); `/permissions` edits go
    through item 4 only.*
11. **`/rewind` that covers shell side effects and the conversation.** Effort M to L. Touches `internal/checkpoint`,
    `internal/tools/shell`, `internal/agent/exec.go`, `internal/gitx/snapshot.go` (`SnapshotTree` exists). Evidence F8 (CX#11626
    225, CC#353 178). *Security: snapshots copy file contents, secrets included, so they stay in the private state directory (0700,
    hash-verified), skip hard-denied paths, are size-bounded, and restore keeps the manifest vetting that refuses paths outside the root.*
12. **Headless.** Effort M. Full tool output in `tool_end` (today the first line), an `--output-schema` for the final answer, and an
    approval delegate for CI. Touches `internal/session/sink.go` (`JSONSink`), `cmd/sleipnir/run.go`, `internal/perm` (Prompter).
    *Security: an approval delegate is a new approval channel: user config only, never a project file; it sees only the
    harness-built request, not the model's justification text; it fails closed on timeout or error; a model-based approver must not
    see tool results (the design Anthropic describes). Full output in JSON carries whatever the tool printed: treat it like the
    verbatim session log (`docs/SECURITY.md`).*

Not ranked, for the maintainer to decide: ACP or editor integration (CC#6686, 552; Cline IntelliJ 1,193 votes and Zed 160 votes, as transcribed) and
remote control (CX#9224, 544) have high demand but are interface choices. A control surface reachable from the agent's own shell
must never be able to change mode or permissions (the DeepSeek Harness flaw above); `sleipnir inspect` is GET-only and loopback by
default, which is the right shape. Resuming swarm sessions is missing and of medium demand.

## 8. Where the evidence is thin

* **Headless and CI**: per-issue counts are 0 to 5, and several items are known only from search snippets. The pattern (exit 0 on
  failure, hangs, no timeout) repeats across three tools, so I kept it, but it does not support a high rank by itself.
* **Cheap and local models**: many small issues (most 17 or fewer) across four tools; the large ones are two parameter errors
  (CX#17809, OC#13546) and the DeepSeek `reasoning_content` series. Quirks of text-form tool calls are anecdotal.
* **Cost multiplication in swarms**: only CX#9748 (18) and CC#68619 (22) carry counts. The larger figures in (01) (agent teams at
  about 7 times the tokens, 2.6 to 5.9 times for fan-out) are third-party or unreplicated and were not re-checked.
* **Aider, Continue, goose**: small trackers; Aider is dormant. Cline's issues are low in reactions and its Discussions figures
  are unverified transcriptions.
* **Missing sources**: Reddit (blocked), Discord, Cursor, Copilot CLI, Amp, Windsurf, vendor changelogs and docs, and any
  measure of what people do rather than what they ask for.
* **Bias**: reactions accumulate over time, so 2026 issues had less time; "closed" mixes shipped, declined and duplicate; and
  issue trackers over-represent people who hit problems.
* **First-hand tests** used a scripted endpoint and a prebuilt binary, not a real model and not a rebuilt HEAD.
